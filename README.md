# Helping Your Boyfriend

An affectionate boyfriend. A quiet house. A nightly routine that becomes harder to trust.

Helping Your Boyfriend is a psychological-horror visual novel that mixes romantic conversations, doll-work minigames and choices with unsettling consequences. We begin inside an established relationship with Dr. Adrian Winchester, rather than meeting a stranger on a first date. Our protagonist lives with Adrian while dealing with a leg illness, and the evenings revolve around his return from work.

At first, we choose our replies and help take apart old dolls. As the story develops, familiar rooms and reassuring words invite a second look. We decide what to accept, what to question and which details to follow. The soft pink presentation is part of the contrast: this is a horror story, not a promise of a comfortable romance.

## What makes the game interesting?

- **Romance with an unsettling edge.** We read Adrian's attention through everyday conversations, then reconsider what that care means as new scenes unfold.
- **Four story endings.** Earlier choices and the investigation give a replay a purpose beyond pressing different buttons at random. Minigame game-over screens are separate from these four outcomes.
- **Five minigames.** Doll work and timed interactions interrupt the reading. We follow the current tool, sorting or key prompt instead of assuming every task uses the same input.
- **A customisable protagonist.** We choose a name, pronouns and body type before settling into the story. Appearance choices are separate from gameplay difficulty.
- **Characters worth paying attention to.** Adrian is our starting point; Ian and Oliver become important as we discover more. We compare what people say with what we have seen.
- **English and Thai text.** The browser game's opening screen lets us choose a language before entering the story.

## Start playing

1. Open [Home](https://helpingyourboyfriend.org/) and select **Play Now** in the player near the top of the page.
2. Allow the game to finish loading. Opening the player does not mean every game asset is ready; wait for the game's own start screen.
3. Choose English or Thai, then follow the game's character setup and opening instructions.
4. Find Save, Load and Settings in the in-game menu. We keep an early manual save for story choices and a separate checkpoint before a difficult task.
5. Read the first run at our own pace. We can leave the ending walkthrough until we want to compare outcomes.

The buttons beneath the player offer webpage fullscreen, browser fullscreen and, once the player opens, **Reload game**. Reloading can discard unsaved progress. We save inside the game first whenever it is responding.

## A few habits that help

- We read every visible answer before confirming it. A warmer-sounding reply is not automatically the right choice for the route we want.
- We separate story saves from task saves. Loading just before a minigame helps with input practice; changing an earlier relationship choice needs an earlier checkpoint.
- We release skip before unfamiliar dialogue or a new choice. Faster reading is useful on replay, but not if we pass the scene we wanted to compare.
- We watch the task's feedback. During doll work, the selected tool, receiving slot and counters tell us more than repeated clicks.
- We replay with one clear goal. Changing a particular decision is easier to understand than changing every answer at once.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many endings are there?

There are four story endings. An earlier game-over screen after a failed interaction is not a fifth ending. The [Endings page](https://helpingyourboyfriend.org/endings) explains the named outcomes and their connected paths.

### Is this a dating game or a horror game?

It combines romantic presentation with psychological horror. Affectionate dialogue and pastel artwork do not remove the disturbing themes. We check the content warning before starting.

### Which controls do we use first?

Left click or Enter advances dialogue; Space hides or restores the text box, and L opens the dialogue log. Doll work and timed tasks have their own prompts. The [Controls page](https://helpingyourboyfriend.org/controls) separates these interactions so we do not mistake a dialogue shortcut for a minigame instruction.

### What should we do if a timed task is difficult?

Read its prompt, keep a nearby task checkpoint and check whether our build offers a lower gameplay difficulty. Difficulty options can differ between editions. We follow the settings and instructions actually visible in our game.

### Why is the browser player still loading?

The externally hosted game may continue loading after its frame opens. We wait for the start screen rather than treating the page's loading message as proof the game is ready. If loading stalls, **Reload game** restarts the player, with a warning about unsaved progress. An external service outage may still prevent play.

### Can we keep our progress when leaving the page?

We use the game's own save menu before leaving or reloading. This site does not manage the game's save system or guarantee that progress carries between devices, browsers or editions. Clearing browser storage may affect locally stored game data.

### Do we need to read every page before playing?

No. Home is enough to begin, and Controls is useful when an interaction blocks us. Characters and Endings contain later-story information, so we can return to them when we are ready for spoilers.

### Is this the official developer website?

No. This is an independent player companion. Helping Your Boyfriend was created by Zieelane and Mousefishdog. Visit the [official game page on itch.io](https://zieelane.itch.io/helping-your-boyfriend) for original downloads and creator announcements. The separately hosted browser build may differ from the downloadable edition.

## Content warning

Helping Your Boyfriend includes psychological horror, jumpscares, loud sounds, flashing images, blood, cartoon gore, violence, drug use and unhealthy relationships. We start at a comfortable volume and stop if the themes or effects are not a good fit. Lowering gameplay difficulty does not remove the story's horror content.
