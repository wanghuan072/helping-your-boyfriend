## Locked Decisions

- Selected direction: light “clinical case file,” not dark cinematic or generic neon gaming.
- Signature: prescription tab used functionally in Header state, player state, section transitions, and Footer brand rule.
- Colors, type roles, spacing, shape, icon, focus, motion, and z-index tokens are fixed in `design-tokens.json`.
- Game player, recommendation, article, and video remain one full left track; side rail is 400px desktop and 312px at 1024px.
- Player cover uses per-game fit metadata. `contain` is the default for title/edge-sensitive art; `cover` requires context-by-context proof.
- Webpage Fullscreen uses a page rectangle with outward arrows; Browser Fullscreen uses four separated outer corners.
- Both Guides use a useful TOC due to planned section count; it is sticky on wide/1024 and in-flow at 768.
